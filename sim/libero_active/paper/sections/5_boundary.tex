\section{Boundary Analysis: The Readout Rejects Tempting Mechanisms}
\label{sec:boundary}

A natural next step is to explain \emph{why} the prior is anchored and to claim a fix. We
report such an analysis not as a contribution but to show what the readout does when a
tempting mechanistic story is put to it: it rejects the ones that do not hold up.

\paragraph{A defended-prior hypothesis.}
One hypothesis is that anchoring is defended by gradient competition with the canonical
demonstrations. We test it by comparing M2 ($100\%$ synthetic) against N1 (the same
synthetic count \emph{plus} an equal count of canonical demonstrations, at matched training
budget): if canonical demonstrations defend the prior, adding them back should re-anchor
successful reaches. On LIBERO-spatial this direction appears---$\ugap$ drops from $0.43$
(M2) to $0.23$ (N1), a difference of $+0.20$ with a task-cluster $90\%$ interval of
$[-0.01, +0.46]$ (seed-level $[+0.16, +0.24]$). But on LIBERO-object the effect vanishes:
$+0.03$, interval $[-0.15, +0.19]$. In tight object basins the canonical prior structurally
fails---an anchored reach simply does not succeed---so it cannot defend via steering, and
both M2 and N1 must ground. The hypothesis does not generalize.

\paragraph{Grounding beyond a competence-matched null.}
A second hypothesis is that fine-tuning produces grounding \emph{beyond} what a merely
degraded policy would show. We build a competence-matched degradation null---the base
policy under inference-time action noise, tuned to match the intervention's success---and
ask whether the intervention's success-conditioned steering exceeds the null's. On
LIBERO-spatial the direction is positive but underpowered: the pre-registered task-cluster
statistic is $+0.26$ with interval $[-0.10, +0.59]$ (it excludes zero at the seed level,
$[+0.15, +0.32]$). On LIBERO-object it is negative---the matched null grounds \emph{more}
than the intervention (null $\ugap = 0.64$ vs $0.52$), giving $-0.12$, interval
$[-0.40, +0.08]$: in a tight basin every surviving policy must ground, so the intervention
adds nothing beyond degradation-survivorship.

\paragraph{Reading.}
Neither mechanistic claim survives across both suites. This is the point. The robust,
general result is not a mechanism but the readout: it dissociates basin-widening from
grounding, and it is sharp enough to reject explanations that success rate would leave
unexamined. Full statistics---the degradation-null construction, the gap statistic, and
the bootstrap procedure---are in the appendix.
