\section{Statistical Details}
\label{app:stats}

\paragraph{Task-cluster bootstrap.}
LIBERO suites contain 10 tasks, which we treat as clusters. All intervals are $90\%$
percentile intervals from a nonparametric pairs-cluster bootstrap: we resample the 10 task
clusters with replacement and recompute the median statistic. (This is not the Rademacher
wild-cluster bootstrap, which is defined for regression coefficients rather than medians; a
beta-binomial / logistic mixed model with task random effects is the appropriate
pre-registered inference for a camera-ready version.)

\paragraph{Success-conditioned gap.}
For a condition, $\ugap = \mathrm{median}(\ufail) - \mathrm{median}(\usucc)$ over displaced
rollouts at $50$\,mm, with $\uu$ the pre-grasp endpoint (closest-approach fallback when the
gripper never closes; fallback was not triggered in the reported runs).

\paragraph{Competence-matched degradation null.}
The null is the base policy under inference-time Gaussian action noise added to the
executed end-effector deltas. We search the noise scale so that the null's clean and
displaced success match the intervention arm; the matched scales are $\sigma\!\approx\!0.15$
(spatial) and $\sigma\!\approx\!0.20$ (object). The null shares the base policy's grounding
and is degraded only by execution noise, isolating whether the intervention grounds
\emph{beyond} degradation-survivorship. We report the directional gain
$G = \ugap_{\mathrm{int}} - \ugap_{\mathrm{null}}$ with a $\vv$-dispersion guard requiring
the intervention not to be more dispersed than the null.

\paragraph{Seeds.}
Claim-critical fine-tuning arms are run at 3 training seeds; the reported spatial $\ugap$
for the $100\%$-synthetic arm is $0.43 \pm 0.02$ across seeds, indicating the steering
signal is not seed noise.

\section{Second-Policy Integration Notes}
\label{app:2ndpolicy}
We attempted a second-policy datapoint with a from-scratch ACT policy and a pretrained
pi0.5 LIBERO checkpoint. ACT trained (training loss decreased) but evaluated at near-zero
clean success in our rollout harness, consistent with an evaluation-interface mismatch
rather than a training failure; the pi0.5 attempt was repeatedly interrupted by
checkpoint-download and shared-GPU contention. We report these as engineering limitations;
the readout itself is policy-agnostic and imposes no such requirement.
